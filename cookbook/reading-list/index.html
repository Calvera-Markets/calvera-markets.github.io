Reading List

Foundational Systems

  • LMAX Disruptor — the canonical lock-free ring buffer for trading systems. Martin Thompson’s technical paper and mechanical-sympathy blog.
  • Aeron — ultra-low-latency messaging (Java/C++). Martin Thompson’s follow-up to Disruptor.
  • Martin Thompson’s Mechanical Sympathy — Google Group and blog on hardware-aware system design.
  • Gil Tene’s HDR Histogram — latency measurement done right. Full distribution with minimal memory.

Market Microstructure (Academic)

  • Maureen O’Hara — Market Microstructure Theory
  • Glosten-Milgrom model — adverse selection in market making
  • Kyle (1985) — informed trading and market impact
  • Menkveld (2013) — “High Frequency Trading and the New Market Makers”
  • Budish, Cramton & Shim (2015) — “The High-Frequency Trading Arms Race: Frequent Batch Auctions as a Market Design Response”

Practitioner Books

  • Barry Johnson — Algorithmic Trading and DMA — the practitioner’s bible
  • Rishi Narang — Inside the Black Box — how quant strategies actually work
  • Michael Lewis — Flash Boys — narrative, not technical, but culturally important

Exchange Specifications

  • CME: MDP 3.0 (market data), iLink (order entry)
  • NASDAQ: ITCH (market data), OUCH (order entry)
  • FIX Protocol specification — industry standard for order entry
  • Binance / Bybit / Deribit API documentation — crypto-specific
  • LSE MIT, CBOE, ICE — European and options exchange protocols

DeFi / MEV

  • Daian et al. — Flash Boys 2.0 (MEV in DeFi)
  • Cyclic Arbitrage in Decentralized Exchanges — DEX arb mechanics
  • Flashbots research papers — MEV extraction and mitigation

Tools & Frameworks

  • DPDK — kernel-bypass networking (Intel)
  • Solarflare OpenOnload / ef_vi — kernel-bypass NIC APIs
  • hftbacktest — microstructure-aware backtesting (Rust/Python)
  • HFTPerformance — open-source benchmark framework for trading pipelines

Hardware & FPGA

  • Papers on FPGA-accelerated trading (Xilinx/AMD, Intel Stratix)
  • Intel/AMD PMU documentation for hardware performance counters
  • PTP (Precision Time Protocol) for cross-host clock synchronization
